Put the PDFs you want to chat with in this folder.

Good ones to start with (free on arxiv.org, click "View PDF" then save):

  - Attention Is All You Need (transformers)        https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762
  - CLIP: Learning Transferable Visual Models        https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00020
  - LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of LLMs              https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685
  - Retrieval-Augmented Generation (the RAG paper)  https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401

Rename them to something short (attention.pdf, clip.pdf, lora.pdf, rag.pdf), it makes the
/focus and /summary commands of the mini project easier to use.

LoRA will come back in pillar 4 when we fine-tune a model, so it's a good one to have.
