# LabelCo - FAQ for customers

LabelCo is our image labeling vendor. This FAQ was downloaded from their customer portal.

## Turnaround

Standard turnaround is 3 business days for batches up to 5,000 images. Express (24 h) costs
40% more. Batches above 20,000 images are split automatically.

## Pricing

Bounding boxes: 0.04 EUR per image. Segmentation masks: 0.12 EUR per image. Volume discounts
start at 50,000 images per quarter.

## Quality

Every batch is double-checked on a 5% sample. If the error rate on the sample is above 2%,
the batch is relabeled for free.

## Data processing

Images are stored for 30 days on servers in the EU, then deleted.
